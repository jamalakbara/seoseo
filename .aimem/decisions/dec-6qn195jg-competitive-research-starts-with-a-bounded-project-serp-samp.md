The first M5 domain overview samples at most 500 distinct project keywords per market, groups registrable domains and counts the best result once per keyword. Newest snapshots win even when empty or metasearch. This prevents stale positive rankings and avoids exposing other projects keyword lists. REST, UI and MCP share one domain service; no provider requests or migrations. Broad domain indexing, backlink discovery and AI visibility remain later work.
